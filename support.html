Frequently Asked Questions

What is Gallery Cleaner Swipe Photos? +
A focused gallery cleanup app for iPhone. You pick a category — screenshots, similar photos, large files, or your full camera roll — and review items one at a time with a simple swipe flow. Nothing is deleted until you confirm a final review step.
Is the app free? +
Yes. The app is free to download and the core swipe-to-review flow is included. The Pro upgrade unlocks unlimited cleaning, faster review flows, and advanced tools for managing screenshots, similar photos, and large files.
What does "swipe to review before delete" actually mean? +
When you swipe left on a photo, it goes into an in-app review basket — it is not deleted yet. After you finish a session, you see the full list of items marked for removal and can confirm or change your mind on any of them. Only after the final confirm step does the app actually remove the photos.
Are deleted photos gone forever? +
No. Photos removed through the app go to your iOS Recently Deleted album, where they stay for 30 days by default. You can restore anything from Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted during that window.
How do you find similar photos and large files? +
Detection runs entirely on your device. The app scans your library locally to surface visually similar shots (so you can pick the best one) and to highlight large videos and media files that take up disproportionate storage.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere? +
No. Photos are read on-device only and are never uploaded to a server we operate. The app processes everything locally on your iPhone — see the privacy policy for details.
Why does the app ask for photo library access? +
It needs access to read the items you want to review. You can grant full access or choose Limited access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos — the app works either way, but full access lets it surface all your screenshots, similar photos, and large files at once.
What does Pro unlock? +
Pro unlocks unlimited cleaning sessions, faster review flows, and advanced tools for managing screenshots, similar photos, and large files. If you have a heavy camera roll to work through, Pro removes the daily limits so you can finish in one sitting.
How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone? +
Sign in to the new device with the same Apple ID, install Gallery Cleaner Swipe Photos, and tap "Restore Purchases" inside the app. Apple's App Store handles entitlement — no separate account needed.
I deleted something by mistake. How do I get it back? +
Open the iOS Photos app, go to Albums → Recently Deleted, and restore the item. iOS keeps deleted photos there for 30 days. Beyond that window, restoration depends on your iCloud Photos backup settings.
What devices and iOS versions are supported? +
iPhone running a recent iOS version. Check the App Store listing for the current minimum iOS requirement.
How do I cancel or refund a Pro purchase? +
Subscriptions and purchases are managed through Apple. Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on iPhone to manage, or request a refund through reportaproblem.apple.com under Apple's standard refund policy.

Why this app

Less clutter.
More space for the photos that matter.

Old screenshots, duplicate-looking photos, and heavy files quietly fill your device. This app helps you find them, review them, and clear them with confidence.

What this isn't

  • An auto-delete tool that decides for you
  • A cloud uploader that copies your gallery
  • A photo editor or filter app
  • An album or face-recognition organizer
  • A backup or recovery service

What it is

  • A swipe-based gallery review flow
  • A fast way to clear screenshots and large files
  • A similar-photo cleaner you stay in control of
  • A storage helper with a final confirmation step
  • A clean, focused tool — not a bloated suite
Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Six focused tools that help you clear clutter, free up space, and keep your camera roll organized.

Free up storage faster

Find what takes up space, review it quickly, and keep your gallery easier to manage. Old screenshots and heavy files won't pile up the way they used to.

Clean screenshots fast

Screenshots pile up fast. Clear saved pages, receipts, chats, and other screen captures you no longer need — make space for the photos that matter more.

Swipe to review before delete

Use a simple swipe flow to go through items one by one. Keep what matters, remove what does not, and stay in control through the full review process.

Find large files

Large videos and media files can take more storage than expected. Surface big items quickly so you can decide what to keep and what to remove.

Remove similar photos

Review similar photos faster and decide which shots deserve space in your gallery. Keep the best ones and clear the rest with confidence.

Review with control

Nothing is deleted without your confirmation. You can check everything before the final delete step, so your memories stay in your hands.

How It Works

From cluttered
to clean in four steps.

No accounts. No setup. Open the app and start clearing space.

01

Pick a category

Screenshots, similar photos, large files, or your full camera roll — choose what you want to review.

02

Swipe to decide

Swipe right to keep, left to remove. Go through items one at a time at your own pace.

03

Check the basket

Everything you marked for removal lands in a review basket. Nothing is deleted yet.

04

Confirm to delete

Review the final list, confirm the delete step, and free up the space you just cleared.

Stay in control

Three things you
won't lose sleep over.

Photo cleaners have a reputation for being aggressive. Here's how this one isn't.

Nothing deletes itself.

Every removal goes through a final confirmation step. Your photos and memories stay in your hands the whole way through.

Recoverable for 30 days.

Items deleted from the app go to your iOS Recently Deleted album, where you can restore anything you change your mind about.

One simple flow.

Swipe, basket, confirm. No menus to dig through, no ten-step wizards — just a clean review you can pick up and put down.
